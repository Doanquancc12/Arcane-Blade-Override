## 🔧 Installation & Integration

Getting FangForge up and running is as smooth as silk. We've moved away from complex command-line inputs. The delivery mechanism is a **portable, signature-aware loader** that you simply drag and drop onto your system's root directory.

Once placed, a simple double-click activates the **Harmonic Resonance Link**, which connects the toolkit to your game's client during the boot-up sequence. The entire process is designed to be **invisible and non-intrusive**, leaving no trace on your system's permanent storage unless you choose to save a configuration profile.

> **Note:** This tool is built for the latest build version (v2.71). A dynamic updater is bundled to ensure compatibility with future patches.

## 📊 Performance Metrics & Stability

We don't just throw code at a wall and see what sticks. FangForge is heavily **tested and optimized** for *zero-latency operation*. The memory footprint is minuscule (less than 3 MB of RAM), and the CPU overhead is negligible under 1%. This means your game's frame rate stays locked at your monitor's refresh rate, even with all visual overlay elements active. The codebase is written in a low-level language to avoid garbage collection hitches, ensuring that your immersion is never broken by a stutter.

## ⚖️ The Ethical Compass

Let's be absolutely clear: FangForge is a **single-player experience enhancer**. It is designed to allow you to explore the game's world, narrative, and mechanics without the artificial frustration of arbitrary timers and grind walls. It is **not** intended for use in competitive multiplayer lobbies where you would gain an unfair advantage over other human players.

We respect the game's original code and architecture. FangForge operates on a **sandboxed principle**, reading and writing only to the memory space designated for your local session. It does not intercept network packets, nor does it modify the game's external servers. You are simply reshaping your local copy of the universe.
